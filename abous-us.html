About Us

Couple Prompts is a simple site for one thing: AI prompts for couple photos. You pick one, copy it, and use it on Gemini, ChatGPT, or any AI tool you like.

We try prompts ourselves before putting them up. So what you copy has actually worked, not just something we typed and hoped for.

What's on here

  • Romantic and aesthetic couple prompts
  • Traditional and festival prompts
  • Cinematic and retro-style prompts
  • Whatever's trending at the moment

No need to know anything about writing prompts. Pick a style, copy it, upload your photo, done.

Is it free?

Yes, every prompt here is free. One thing to keep in mind — the result depends on which AI tool you use and your original photo, so it won't come out exactly the same every time.

Why this site exists

Good prompts float around on Instagram, WhatsApp and group chats and then just vanish. We wanted a place to keep the good ones together so you're not searching from scratch every time.

We're not related to Google, Gemini, OpenAI, ChatGPT or any AI company. Just a site that uses their tools.
