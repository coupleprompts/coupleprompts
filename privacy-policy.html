Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

At Couple Prompts, accessible at coupleprompts.in, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be collected when you use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Couple Prompts is primarily a content website. You do not need to create an account to browse or copy any prompt listed here.

Depending on how you use the site, we or our service providers may automatically receive information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search terms
  • General, non-precise location (such as country or city)

We do not ask visitors for sensitive personal information, and nothing on this site requires you to submit a personal photo to us — any photo you use with an AI tool stays between you and that tool.

2. Information You Provide to Us

If you contact us directly, for example by email, we may receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We use this only to respond to your query, provide support, or communicate with you about your request.

3. Cookies

Couple Prompts may use cookies and similar technologies to help the site function properly, understand how visitors use it, and support advertising or analytics services.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by the websites you visit. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; some parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

This site may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also review how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites or apps through Google's own privacy policy.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use this website, such as which pages are viewed most and how people navigate the site. This helps us improve the content and overall experience. Analytics data is generally aggregated and not used to personally identify visitors.

6. Third-Party Links and AI Tools

This website contains prompts intended for use with third-party AI tools such as Google Gemini and ChatGPT, and may link to those or other external websites.

Once you leave Couple Prompts, the privacy policy and terms of that third-party site or tool apply. We do not control, and are not responsible for, how any third-party AI tool handles photos or data you choose to upload there.

7. Children's Privacy

Couple Prompts does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect any information provided to us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding information collected about you. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and manage ad personalisation through the advertising controls provided by Google and other networks.
